Tase — Privacy Policy

App: Tase — Sound Meter & Tools (Android, package io.github.alexest.tase) · Effective date: 17.10.2026 · Last updated: 17.10.2026

Tase is a measuring app that uses your phone's microphone, accelerometer and ambient light sensor to show sound level, vibration and illuminance, and lets you keep a journal of recordings on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles, what stays on your device, and what the third-party services it includes may collect.

1. Summary

  • Tase has no account, no sign-up and no server of its own. Your measurements, notes, photos and locations are stored only on your device and are never uploaded by the app.
  • The microphone is used to compute sound levels in real time. Audio is never recorded, stored or transmitted.
  • Location is used only if you turn on "Add location" for journal entries, and only to attach coordinates to a recording on your device.
  • The app shows banner advertising through Google AdMob and offers one in-app purchase through Google Play Billing. These Google services process some device data as described below.
  • The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own.

Exports (CSV, PDF) are created only when you ask for them and are handed to the app or location you choose (a file manager, email, a cloud folder). What happens after that is governed by that other app's policy.

Deleting a recording in the journal deletes its series, photo and location. Uninstalling the app deletes all app-private data.

4. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising). The free version shows a banner ad. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and information about ad interactions, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before any ad is loaded; you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings. Buying "Remove ads" stops the ads SDK from loading ads.

Google Play Billing (purchase). The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. Tase receives only a purchase token confirming the purchase; it never sees your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy.

No other SDKs, analytics, crash reporters or tracking tools are included.

5. Data sharing and sale

Tase does not sell, share or transfer your measurements, notes, photos or locations to anyone. The only data leaving your device is what the Google services above collect for ads and billing, under their own policies.

6. Data retention

Everything the app stores stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. The developer has no copy and cannot access, restore or delete it for you.

7. Children

Tase is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Security

App data is kept in Android app-private storage, which other apps cannot read. Connections made by the Google services are encrypted (HTTPS).

9. Your rights

Because Tase keeps your data only on your device, you exercise your rights directly: view, export (CSV/PDF), edit or delete any recording in the app. For data processed by Google (ads, billing), use your Google account and device settings, or contact Google. If you are in the EEA/UK and have questions about this policy, contact the developer at the address below.
